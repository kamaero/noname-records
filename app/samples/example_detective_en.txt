Chapter 1: The Empty Drawer

Maya checked the old desk one last time. The brass key had vanished.

“Did anyone come in here?” she asked.

“I saw the caretaker near the door,” Noah said. “He was carrying a blue folder.”

Maya noticed a thread caught beneath the drawer. It was the same color as the folder.

Chapter 2: The Blue Folder

The caretaker opened the folder and smiled.

“I found this on the floor,” he said. “I thought someone might need it.”

Maya held up the brass key. “That solves one mystery,” she said.

“And the other?” Noah asked.

Maya pointed to the thread. “Next time, close the drawer gently.”
