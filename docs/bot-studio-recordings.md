# Recording details in the Telegram studio

Open Studio → Books → a book. Administrators, agents and authors can access
the report; permissions are checked on every callback.

The book card links to roles with no approved actor (including tentative '?'
assignments), approved roles with no accepted takes, and partially recorded
roles. Each entry shows the actor, chapter progress and missing chapter numbers.
Roles with no speaking chapters are excluded.

The overview and details share the same calculation. Takes are matched using
the installation's book and role identity rules, current speaking chapters and
assigned actor. Auditions do not count. Completion describes chapters with
takes; it does not certify ASR coverage of every spoken line.

Long lists are paginated rather than silently truncated. Every callback reads
fresh data. Reports do not change assignments, recordings or deadlines, send
messages to actors, or call paid services.
